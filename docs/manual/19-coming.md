# Coming Soon {#coming}

::: lore
*"Patience. The waiting is the worst."*

— a hero, idle
:::

The game is being built in the open, and some of what it will be is not here yet. This chapter says what is coming, so that you do not go looking for it. Everything in the rest of this book is in the game today.

::: warn "Being built now"
**Horde Mode.** A battle mode of its own: your host holds a piece of ground against wave after wave of the enemy, each stronger than the last, for as long as it can stand. The rules of battle are those of this book: facing, charges, morale and all.

**Capture the Flag.** A battle mode in which each side has a flag to guard and the other's to take: a race of fast troops and a fight for the ground between, played on the same fields.

These two are being made as this edition is written; when they are in the game they will be offered on the title screen, and this chapter will become theirs.
:::

## Online play {#online}

The battle was made from the first day to be played between people: every order is an order to the simulation, which runs exactly the same on every phone and computer, so two players need only send each other their orders. Battles and campaigns against other players, phone against phone over the mobile network, are planned. A player who drops out is covered by computer generals until he comes back.

## Planned for the campaign {#planned-campaign}

- **Ruins fought in 3D:** the hero (and perhaps a companion) against the guardian, in the ruin's own interior, instead of the dice.
- **Temple quests**, with an artifact for reward.
- **Razing** a city, and a new capital chosen when the old one falls.
- **Hatreds:** computer lords with attitudes to each of the others.
- **Splitting a stack** by hand.
- **Generals:** handing an army to the computer to command (hold, skirmish, assault, retreat at a given loss), and several battles across the continent at once.
- **The land remembers:** the dead, the arrows, the craters and the burnt houses of every battle staying on the land, weathering slowly through the war.
- **Choosing your realm** when the campaign begins.

## Planned for the battle {#planned-battle}

- Walls breached and woods burnt.
- Reinforcements arriving during a battle.
- Weather: rain and snow (the sounds of rain and thunder are already made).
