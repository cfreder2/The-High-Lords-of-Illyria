# Morale {#morale}

::: lore
*"Run! Run!"*

*"Back to the hills!"*

*"Every dwarf for himself!"*

— the Iron Hill Legion, at the Sorrow Ford
:::

Every soldier has **morale**, from 1000 (fresh and willing) down to nothing. Shocks take it away; time, friends and a hero bring it back. When it runs low he **wavers**; lower still, he **breaks** and runs. A battle is lost, more often than not, by the side that breaks first, not by the side that dies first.

## Shocks {#shocks}

| Shock | Morale lost |
|---|---|
| A hero of his own side falls within 30 m | 850 |
| ...within 60 m | 425 |
| A friend killed within 12 m | 70 |
| A blow in the flank / in the rear | 25 / 50 (missiles half) |
| Being charged | 150, more in the flank or rear |
| Outnumbered: every third of a second, for each foe within 15 m beyond the friends there (counting himself) | 10 |

Each kind of soldier takes shocks differently: the **morale** figure in the compendium scales them, against a man's 100% (light infantry 110%, heavy infantry 85%, berserkers 35%, devils 20%, elephants 120%). **Undead and heroes never break.** Each step of veterancy takes 3% off every shock.

## Wavering and breaking {#breaking}

- **Below 450** a soldier **wavers**: his blows and reloads come a quarter slower and his aim is half again as wide. His health bar has an amber frame, and you may hear it in his voice.
- **Below 200** he **breaks**: he drops his orders, ignores new ones and runs from the enemies within 40 m. His bar shows a white flag. You cannot order him; the computer does not try to order a squad whose men have all broken.
- A broken man **rallies**, standing and ready for orders again, once his morale is back to **550** and no enemy is within **25 m** of him.

A hero's fall in front of green light infantry breaks them all at once. Elite heavy infantry are badly shaken by it, but hold, and rally in about eight seconds once the danger has passed.

## Recovering {#recovering}

After two seconds without a shock, and not outnumbered, a soldier's morale comes back at **45 a second**, **+45** more near a living hero of his side (within 30 m), and **+6** for each friend near him (up to six).

**Veterans come back first.** Each step of veterancy adds a quarter to his recovery, lowers his rally bar by 50 and the foe-free distance he needs by 3 m:

| Rank | Rallies at | Needs no foe within | Alone and safe, rallies in about |
|---|---|---|---|
| Recruit | 550 | 25 m | 14 s |
| Regular | 500 | 22 m | |
| Veteran | 450 | 19 m | 8.7 s |
| Elite | 400 | 16 m | 7 s |

## Steadied by veterans {#steadied}

A soldier counts the veterans and heroes near him who are not shaken themselves. Each veteran of a higher grade than his within **12 m** takes **4%** off his shocks (an elite **6%**); a hero of his side within 30 m **15%**; together at most **25%**. Half as much again goes into his recovery. Recruits under the same hail of shocks hold about half again as long beside one elite. Mixing a few veterans into a green line is worth more than the veterans alone.

A hero's fall cancels the steadiness at once, which is why his death still breaks green troops.

## "Hold the line!" {#hold-the-line}

A **veteran** or **elite** who is not wavering himself, and sees at least **two** friends within 20 m wavering or broken, calls out: "*Hold the line!*", "*Stand fast!*", "*Shields up, hold!*" A **hero** calls when he sees even **one**: "*Rally to me!*" Every friend in earshot (20 m) gains morale:

| Caller | Morale given |
|---|---|
| A veteran | +150 |
| An elite | +200 |
| A hero | +300 |

A broken man whose morale so reaches the waver line (450, or his own lower rally bar) **turns back at once**, even with enemies near. Then the caller is quiet for 15 seconds (a hero 12).

## Heroes in battle {#heroes-in-battle}

A hero is a squad on his own: 260 hit points at level 1 and 8% more a level, a blow of 30 (and 6% more a level), a big shield, and blows every 1.1 s. He **never breaks**. Every soldier of his side within **30 m** fights with his leadership edge, takes 15% less from every shock, and recovers 45 morale a second faster; and he calls the broken back.

The price of all that is his death. A hero who falls takes 850 morale from every soldier of his side within 30 m, and 425 from those within 60 m: green troops near him break where they stand. Keep your hero behind the first line, close enough to lead and to rally, never alone at the front. The enemy's hero is worth the same to you: kill him, and watch his line. (In Horde the blow is the same, but the hero himself comes back between the waves: chapter [](#hero-returns).)

::: tip
The two ways to beat a stronger army in 3D are to **break it** and to **flank it**. A charge into the flank shakes more than a charge into the front; a blow in the back does twice the damage and costs the man struck twice the morale; and a hero's fall does more than any of it.
:::
