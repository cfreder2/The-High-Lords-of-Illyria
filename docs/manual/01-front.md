# About This Book {#about}

This is the manual of *The High Lords of Illyria*, a war for a continent fought on two layers at once. Above, a campaign after the old *Warlords*: eight lords, eighty walled cities, stacks of armies marching on roads and fording rivers, heroes searching ruins, gold that never stretches far enough. Below, wherever two armies meet, a battle after *Myth*: every soldier a figure on the real ground, every arrow a falling thing that can miss and hit a friend, every blow landing on a shield, a flank or a back.

You do not need to read the whole book to play. Chapter Two gets you onto the field in five minutes and into the campaign in ten. Come back to the rest when you want to know why your knights lost their charge in the wood, why the archers on the walls outshoot yours, or what the Hermit of the Peaks will tell a hero who climbs to him.

## Turning the pages

- **On a phone:** swipe left for the next page and right for the previous one; the page follows your finger. A tap on the right third of the page turns forward, on the left third back. A tap in the middle shows the bar: the contents (☰), search (⌕), the page counter, larger or smaller type (A−, A+) and light or dark pages (◐).
- **On a desktop:** the arrow keys, Page Up and Page Down, the space bar, a click on either side of the page, or the mouse wheel. [[Home]] and [[End]] go to the first and last page, [[T]] to the contents and [[/]] to search.
- **Cross references** look like this: [](#gold). Tap one to go there; the ↩ button in the bar brings you back.
- The book remembers the page you were on.

The numbers in this book are the game's own: the unit tables in the compendium are read from the game's locked unit sheet each time the book opens, and the rest were checked against the code when this edition was written. Where something is planned but not yet built, the book says so plainly (chapter [](#coming)).
