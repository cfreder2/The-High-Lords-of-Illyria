# Heroes, Ruins and Temples {#heroes}

::: lore
*"Sir Gawen went into the Weeping Crypt with a lantern and a borrowed sword. He came out with neither, and with the Crown of Kings on his head."*

— the news, day 31
:::

Heroes are what make the war more than a contest of production. A hero leads a stack, and makes every army in it fight a little better; carries artifacts that make it fight a great deal better; and, above all, **explores**. The land is full of places a hero may search: ruins with their guardians and their treasure, temples that bless armies, and two sages who know where the treasure lies.

## Heroes {#hero-rules}

Every lord begins with one hero, its lord, named for its capital (*Lord of Kingshold*), with an escort of light infantry and the realm's own rare troops. More heroes come by **hire** (below). A hero:

- is an army of one, strength **3 + his level**;
- rises a level with kills, in the dice and in 3D: 3 kills for level 2, then 7, 12, 18, 25, 33, 42 and 52 for level 9;
- **leads** his stack: the best hero in a stack gives every army in it the *hero leads* edge (+2% at strength 4 to 6, +4% at 7 or 8, +6% at 9), and on the battlefield the same to every soldier of his side within 30 m of him;
- carries any number of **artifacts**, which go where he goes and fall where he falls;
- draws no upkeep;
- **never breaks** in battle (chapter [](#morale)).

A hero who dies is gone for good. What he carried lies where he fell, and the next hero of any side to enter that cell takes it up ("*Dame Rowena took up the Sunblade*").

## Searching {#searching}

A stack standing still on a ruin, a temple or a sage shows a button at the bottom of the screen: **Search** (a ruin), **Visit** (a temple) or **Consult** (a sage). Ruins and sages need a hero in the stack; the strongest hero in it goes in. A temple will bless any stack.

## Ruins {#ruins}

There are 34 ruins in Illyria, in the forests and the foothills, with names like the *Sunken Halls*, the *Weeping Crypt* and the *Howling Spire*, and one more, the **Drowned Abbey**, on its isle in the Heartmere. Each holds a **guardian**, and the hero goes in **alone** to fight it with the *Warlords* dice: a d10 each against the other's strength, a hit on the low roller, two hits and it is over.

| Guardian | Strength |
|---|---|
| a pack of wolves | 2 |
| a band of brigands | 3 |
| a troll | 4 |
| the restless dead | 5 |
| a giant spider | 5 |
| a minotaur | 6 |
| a demon | 7 |
| an ancient wyrm | 8 |

Guardians are fiercer the further the ruin lies from the nearest capital: a young hero finds an easy ruin near home and a hard one in the wilds. A hero of strength 4 against a troll (4) is a coin toss; against wolves (2), about four times in five.

**A hero who falls** in a ruin is gone, and what he carried lies in the ruin for the next hero; the guardian waits on. **A hero who wins** gains the guardian's strength in kills (and maybe a level), and finds one of these:

- **Gold** (four times in ten): 50 + 25 for each point of the guardian's strength + up to 50 more; wolves leave 100 to 150, a wyrm 250 to 300.
- **An artifact** (a little over a third of the time): one that has not yet been found. Weak guardians (strength 3 or less) guard only moderate treasures (+1), middling ones (4 or 5) up to considerable ones (+2), the fiercest anything up to the supreme (+3).
- **Allies** (a quarter of the time, if the stack has room): undead behind the weakest guardians; undead or wizards behind middling ones; devils or archons; then dragons or demons behind the fiercest. Allies join the hero's stack and want no pay. Sometimes two bands come.

Each ruin is searched once; after that it lies empty for good, greyed on the map.

## Artifacts {#artifacts}

Fifteen artifacts lie hidden in Illyria, each unique. **Battle** artifacts add to the hero's own strength. **Command** artifacts add whole points to the strength of every army fighting beside the hero, as the *Warlords* command modifier did. Two more serve the road and the purse.

| Artifact | Kind | Worth |
|---|---|---|
| Firebrand | battle | +1 |
| Frostblade | battle | +1 |
| Spear of Ashkar | battle | +1 |
| Sunblade | battle | +2 |
| Shadowblade | battle | +2 |
| Bow of the Morning Star | battle | +2 |
| Staff of Thunders | battle | +3 |
| Crown of Kings | command | +1 |
| Sceptre of Dawn | command | +1 |
| Scarlet Banner | command | +1 |
| Ring of Dominion | command | +2 |
| Horn of the North | command | +2 |
| Rod of the High Lords | command | +3 |
| Winged Sandals | the road | +4 movement points a day for the stack |
| Purse of Plenty | the purse | 10 gold a day |

Artifacts show on the hero's card (✧). In the 3D battle a hero's battle artifacts count like blessings, each a little more damage, quicker blows and a little less hurt taken.

::: tip
A command artifact is worth more than a battle one: the Crown of Kings adds a point to every army in the stack, the Sunblade two to one hero. A hero with the Rod of the High Lords makes a stack of eight light infantry fight like heavy infantry.
:::

## Temples {#temples}

Four temples stand in Illyria, one in each quarter of the land: the **Temple of the Dawn**, the **Temple of the Tides**, the **Temple of the Stars** and the **Temple of the Oak**. As in *Warlords*, a temple blesses **any stack**, hero or none: every army in it that has not knelt there before gains a **blessing**, +1 strength (shown ✦). An army can carry a blessing from each temple, four at most, never past strength 9. Blessings go with the army into the 3D battle, where each counts like a rank and a little more: harder blows, quicker hands, steadier nerves.

Each temple also has its **guard**, holy troops who follow the **first hero** to come and kneel there, blessed and serving for nothing:

| Temple | Its guard |
|---|---|
| The Temple of the Dawn | archons |
| The Temple of the Tides | two bands of journeymen |
| The Temple of the Stars | unicorns |
| The Temple of the Oak | pegasi |

(The guard needs room in the stack.) A stack holding a temple, its defenders standing on the holy ground, fights with the *holding* edge.

## Sages {#sages}

Two sages live in Illyria, each in a strange place: the **Seer's Tower** on the Isle of the Seer off the north-west coast, reached only by sea or on wings, and the **Hermit of the Peaks**, high in the Ironveil. The first hero to consult each is given **100 to 150 gold** and told the name of the nearest ruin still unexplored. First come, first served.

## Heroes for hire {#hire}

Now and then, at dawn, a hero offers his sword to a lord who holds **more than two cities for each hero** it already has (one chance in twelve each day). He waits in one of your cities and asks **150 gold, and 100 more for every hero already in your service**, paid now. One time in three he brings a band of rare troops with him (griffins, pegasi, unicorns, wizards, berserkers or dwarven grenadiers), who serve for nothing, and asks 50 gold more.

The offer appears as a prompt: **Hire for 270 gold** or **Decline**. It stands until the next dawn. You cannot hire while an enemy is raising a banner over the town where he waits. A lord whose heroes have all fallen is offered another in time. The heroes of Illyria include Sir Aldric, Dame Isolde, Brannoc the Bold, Lady Mirelle, Torvald Ironhand, Elowen Swiftbow, Kaelan the Grey and Ulfric Bearsbane, and when they have all been hired, their sons and daughters.
