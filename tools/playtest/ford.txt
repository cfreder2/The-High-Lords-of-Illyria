# Example 1: Skirmish at Oakhaven Ford. Your host stands west of the river, the other host east of it, on ground that
# is its mirror image. The flag is raised on the isle in the ford; a bridge crosses 96 m up stream and 96 m down.
# Plan: two squads of heavy infantry to the isle, the archers to the bank above the ford, a squad of pikes to our end
# of each bridge, the heavy cavalry over the upstream bridge; at 40 s the foot (and the warriors) at whoever is at the
# pole; a shot every 10 s of game time for 100 s, with close looks at the isle and the bridge; the numbers at the end. The flag's line is in the log (`capture=`).
#
#   Illyria --play tools/playtest/ford.txt --play-out out/ford --play-log out/ford/ford.log
#
# The coordinates are window points at 1600 x 900 with the camera set below (north is up the screen; the pole is at
# (512, 512) in battle metres, the middle of the window). They were read off pictures taken with it: if you change
# the camera or the size, take `shot` again and move them. The cards are in the order `cards` prints.

size 1600 900
start skirmish ford budget 300
wait 0.5
camera 512 512 330 1.5708
wait 1
shot ford-00-start
cards
stats

# The isle: Heavy Infantry 1 and 2.
select 0
click right 800 440
select 1
click right 800 464
# The bank above the ford: the three squads of archers.
select 9
click right 640 450
select 10
click right 640 400
select 12
click right 640 505
# Our end of each bridge: the pikes.
select 3
click right 690 215
select 4
click right 678 768
# Round by the upstream bridge: the heavy cavalry, to the far bank.
select 7
click right 965 215
wait 0.5
shot ford-01-ordered

wait_ticks 300
shot ford-10
stats
wait_ticks 300
shot ford-20
stats
wait_ticks 300
shot ford-30
camera 512 512 70 1.5708
wait 0.6
shot ford-30-isle
camera 512 512 330 1.5708
stats
# The cavalry is on the upstream bridge about now: a close look at its far end, where their pikes stand.
wait_ticks 225
camera 540 608 55 1.5708
wait 0.5
shot ford-38-bridge
wait_ticks 45
shot ford-39-bridge
camera 512 512 330 1.5708
wait_ticks 30
shot ford-40
stats
# The other host is at the pole by now: at them (a right click on a foe is an attack), the warriors with the rest.
select 0
click right 798 452
select 1
click right 798 452
select 5
click right 798 452
wait_ticks 300
shot ford-50
camera 512 512 70 1.5708
wait 0.6
shot ford-50-isle
camera 512 512 330 1.5708
stats
wait_ticks 300
shot ford-60
stats
wait_ticks 300
shot ford-70
camera 512 512 70 1.5708
wait 0.6
shot ford-70-isle
camera 512 512 330 1.5708
stats
wait_ticks 300
shot ford-80
stats
wait_ticks 300
shot ford-90
stats
wait_ticks 300
shot ford-100
camera 512 512 70 1.5708
wait 0.6
shot ford-100-isle
stats
cards
