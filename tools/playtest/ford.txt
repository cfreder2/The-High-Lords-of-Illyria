# Example 1: Skirmish at the Ford. Your army starts west of the river; the Red Hand are across it, to the east.
# Plan: everyone forms one line on the near bank facing east; then a box round the middle of the line crosses the
# ford; a shot every 10 s of game time for 90 s; the numbers at the end.
#
#   Illyria --play tools/playtest/ford.txt --play-out out/ford --play-log out/ford/ford.log
#
# The coordinates are window points at 1600 x 900 with the camera set below (north is up the screen). They were read off
# pictures taken with it: if you change the camera or the size, take `shot` again and move them.

size 1600 900
start skirmish ford
wait 0.5
camera 520 500 230 1.592
wait 1
shot ford-00-start
cards
stats

# Everyone: select all, then a right-button drag (held still first, as a march with a facing) from the ford's near
# bank eastward: the army marches there and ends in a line facing east.
select all
drag right 640 420 740 420 0.6
wait_ticks 600
shot ford-01-line
stats

# A box round the middle of the line, and a march across the ford onto the far bank.
drag left 420 300 720 520
wait 0.3
shot ford-02-box
click right 950 410
wait 0.5
shot ford-03-crossing

# Let the game run: a shot every 10 s of game time (300 ticks) for 90 s.
wait_ticks 300
shot ford-10
stats
wait_ticks 300
shot ford-20
stats
wait_ticks 300
shot ford-30
stats
wait_ticks 300
shot ford-40
stats
wait_ticks 300
shot ford-50
stats
wait_ticks 300
shot ford-60
stats
wait_ticks 300
shot ford-70
stats
wait_ticks 300
shot ford-80
stats
wait_ticks 300
shot ford-90
stats
cards
quit
