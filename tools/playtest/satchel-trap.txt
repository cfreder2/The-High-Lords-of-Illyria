# The satchel trap at Oakhaven Ford (armies bought for 300 gold a side), as a person would play it. The isle in the
# ford is where both hosts must come: the pole is on it.
#   The deployment pause: the dwarf's five charges are laid in a line across the isle, west of the pole, within a
#   Molotov's throw of your bank (charges laid in the deployment lie there when the battle begins; a click in the ford
#   itself is refused: "Not in water"). He holds his fire and is sent down to your bank of the ford.
#   The battle: when the Red Hand's foot stand on the isle raising their flag: the dwarf, L, click a charge. One bottle,
#   lobbed onto the line.
#
#   Illyria --play tools/playtest/satchel-trap.txt --play-out out/trap --play-log out/trap/trap.log
#
# Compare with satchel-no-trap.txt (the same, no charges). The log's `event=` carries "Ambush: N down"; kills a satchel
# is that N over the five laid. Coordinates are window points at 1600 x 900 with the camera below (north up).

size 1600 900
start skirmish ford budget 300 deploy
wait 0.5
camera 498 510 55 1.5708
wait 1.5
cards

# The dwarf (card 12): T, then Shift+click. The first click is in the ford: refused. Then five across the isle.
select 12
key t
key shift down
click left 760 400
click left 1050 300
click left 1050 348
click left 1050 396
click left 1050 444
click left 1050 492
key shift up
key escape
key shift down
key k
key shift up
# ... and down to your bank of the ford, a throw from the line.
click right 705 400
wait 0.5
shot trap-01-deployed

# Begin.
key return
speed 4
wait_ticks 60
select 12
wait 0.3
shot trap-02-laid
stats

# A minute in the Red Hand's foot stand on the isle raising their flag, and the dwarf is at the bank: L, click a charge.
wait_ticks 1840
stats
select 12
shot trap-03-on-the-isle
key l
click left 1050 396
wait_ticks 150
shot trap-04-blast
stats
wait_ticks 300
shot trap-05-after
stats
wait_ticks 600
stats
cards
quit
