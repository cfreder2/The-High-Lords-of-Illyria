# The satchel trap at the Ford (armies bought for 300 gold a side: foot, bowmen, horse, one dwarf each, engines).
# Your one dwarf mines the ground between the ford and your line, falls back to the pikes, and sets the charges off
# when the Red Hand is on them (his own judgement: two or more foes on a charge and none of yours; a Detonate order,
# L then a click, does it at once whoever stands there: satchel-careless.txt).
#
#   Illyria --play tools/playtest/satchel-trap.txt --play-out out/trap --play-log out/trap/trap.log
#
# Compare with satchel-no-trap.txt (the same waits, the pikes moved the same, the dwarf told nothing): `stats` and the
# log's `foe=` at the same ticks, and the log's event line ("Ambush: N down"). Coordinates are window points at
# 1600 x 900 with the camera below (north up). Satchels are dead in water: the line is laid on the bank, not in the ford.
# The foe's horse ride for the dwarf first: leave him alone on the bank and he is dead before the foot arrive.

size 1600 900
start skirmish ford budget 300
wait 0.5
camera 476 506 45 1.5708
wait 1.5
cards

# The pikes (card 2) forward a little, to cover him.
select 2
click right 300 480

# The dwarf (card 8): T, then Shift+click three places across the way from the ford to your line, each inside the next
# one's blast (the ring): a line. Shift+right-click: then back beside the pikes, within his throw of the nearest.
select 8
key t
key shift down
click left 700 380
click left 630 420
click left 560 460
click right 350 450
key shift up
wait 0.5
shot trap-01-ordered
speed 4
wait_ticks 1500
select 8
wait 0.3
shot trap-02-laid
cards
stats

# The Red Hand comes over the ford.
wait_ticks 500
stats
wait_ticks 200
shot trap-03-t2300
stats
wait_ticks 200
stats
wait_ticks 200
shot trap-04-t2700
stats
wait_ticks 1300
stats
wait_ticks 1000
shot trap-05-t5000
stats
wait_ticks 1000
stats
wait_ticks 1000
shot trap-06-t7000
stats
cards
quit
