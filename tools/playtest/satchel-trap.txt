# The satchel trap at the Ford (armies bought for 300 gold a side: foot, bowmen, horse, one dwarf each, engines), as
# a person would play it.
#   The deployment pause: the dwarf's five charges are laid in a line on your bank where the ford comes up out of the
#   water (charges laid in the deployment lie there when the battle begins); he stays in the rear and holds his fire;
#   the pikes go forward to stand a dozen metres short of the line, holding their place.
#   The battle: the Red Hand's horse come first and break on the pikes. As its foot start across the ford: the dwarf,
#   L, click a charge. He walks up to his throw and lobs his bottle onto the line as they come up out of the water.
#
#   Illyria --play tools/playtest/satchel-trap.txt --play-out out/trap --play-log out/trap/trap.log
#
# Compare with satchel-no-trap.txt (the same orders without the charges). The log's `event=` carries "Ambush: N down";
# kills a satchel is that N over the five laid. Coordinates are window points at 1600 x 900 with the camera below
# (north up). Satchels are dead in water: the line is on the bank, not in the ford.

size 1600 900
start skirmish ford budget 300 deploy
wait 0.5
camera 476 506 45 1.5708
wait 1.5
cards

# The dwarf (card 8): T, then Shift+click five places down the bank, each inside the next one's blast (the ring).
select 8
key t
key shift down
click left 1157 448
click left 1170 480
click left 1185 512
click left 1200 544
click left 1215 576
key shift up
key escape
key shift down
key k
key shift up

# The pikes (card 2) forward, holding their place (K).
select 2
click right 930 505
key k
wait 0.5
shot trap-01-deployed

# Begin.
key return
speed 4
wait_ticks 60
select 8
wait 0.3
shot trap-02-laid
stats

# Their horse come first, twice, and break on the pikes; the dwarf is kept in the rear, out of their way (forward, he is
# the first man they ride for). As their second charge is spent and their foot start across: the dwarf, L, click a
# charge. He has 35 m to walk to his throw, about twenty-five seconds, as long as their foot take to wade the ford.
wait_ticks 1985
stats
select 8
shot trap-03-they-come
key l
click left 1185 512
wait_ticks 600
shot trap-03b-walking
stats
wait_ticks 120
shot trap-04-blast
stats
wait_ticks 180
shot trap-05-after
stats
wait_ticks 300
stats
wait_ticks 300
stats
cards
quit
