#   Illyria --play tools/playtest/boulder-own.txt --play-out ~/Desktop/boulder
#
# `boulder men X Y DX DY N [own]` stands N men who do nothing in a file from (X, Y); `boulder throw X Y TX TY` throws a
# stone as a catapult at (X, Y) would at (TX, TY), with no scatter, and prints the forecast; `boulder count` prints the
# toll and where the stone lies (docs/PLAYTEST.md). The frames are taken at a quarter speed, 5 ticks apart.
