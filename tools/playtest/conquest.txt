# Example 3: Conquest, the first days. You lead the Moonward Order from Moonhaven; the stack standing in the capital is
# picked, sent to the nearest town that is nobody's, at four times the speed, for three minutes of game time
# (5400 ticks, a day and a half), a shot every 20 s.
#
#   Illyria --play tools/playtest/conquest.txt --play-out out/conquest --play-log out/conquest/conquest.log
#
# Coordinates: window points at 1600 x 900, after the wheel turns below have zoomed the map out. With another lord
# (`start conquest lord N`) the capital is elsewhere: look at `shot conquest-00-start` and move them. Nothing is saved.
# (A Conquest's tick starts again from 0 each day, 3600 ticks; wait_ticks counts across that.)

size 1600 900
start conquest
wait_ticks 30
wheel -4
wait 1.5
shot conquest-00-start
stats

# Click the stack's banner (the "6x" on Moonhaven's gate): the stack is picked and the view comes down to it.
click left 763 350
wait 2
shot conquest-01-picked
stats

# A right-click on Wolfsden, the nearest town nobody holds: the dashed line is the route. Then the days speed up.
click right 470 300
wait 1
shot conquest-02-ordered
speed 4
stats

# Every 20 s of game time (600 ticks) for three minutes.
wait_ticks 600
shot conquest-020
stats
wait_ticks 600
shot conquest-040
stats
wait_ticks 600
shot conquest-060
stats
wait_ticks 600
shot conquest-080
stats
wait_ticks 600
shot conquest-100
stats
wait_ticks 600
shot conquest-120
stats
wait_ticks 600
shot conquest-140
stats
wait_ticks 600
shot conquest-160
stats
wait_ticks 600
shot conquest-180
stats
quit
