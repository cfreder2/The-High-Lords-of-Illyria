# west wall, between its two towers. Plan: the siege train (catapults) and the foot go to the gate together; the
# right-click on the gate is an attack on it (the cursor over it says so).

#   Illyria --play tools/playtest/siege.txt --play-out out/siege --play-log out/siege/siege.log

# Coordinates: window points at 1600 x 900, with the camera below. Squad cards (`cards`) here are 4-13: 0-3 are the garrison's.

size 1600 900
start skirmish siege

shot siege-00-start

# The siege train alone first: a click on its card, a march out of the garrison's reach, and look.
